Common questions

No surprises in the scope.

Are these fixed prices?

No. They are proposed starting prices for the listed assumptions. The written quote will account for transaction volume, accounts, software, and reporting needs.

Is QuickBooks included?

Third-party software subscriptions and payroll fees are separate unless your written proposal says otherwise.

What about a large rental portfolio?

Hundreds of units, multiple entities, trust accounting, AppFolio exports, or detailed property reporting call for an individual assessment. The advertised starting price is not a price for a portfolio of any size.

Are tax returns and audits included?

No. These packages focus on bookkeeping and defined business services. Tax preparation, legal advice, and attest or audit services are not included.
